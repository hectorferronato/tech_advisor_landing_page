HOW WE WORK

A focused partner
for consequential choices.

Scheduled advisory conversations and asynchronous reviews share up to four advisor hours per month. Scope, priorities, scheduling, and response expectations are agreed before we start.

Selective hands-on work may help answer a specific question. The engagement purchases independent judgment and targeted involvement; implementation remains with you or your team.

Clear engagement boundaries

This is not a traditional fractional CTO role. It excludes day-to-day engineering management, on-call support, unlimited debugging, continuous development, and production ownership.

A focused review does not certify a system as secure or production-ready. We agree on the limits and next steps for each investigation.

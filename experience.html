WHERE MY APPROACH BEGAN

Resourcefulness,
put into practice.

I grew up in Porto Ferreira, Brazil, and went through the public-school system. In 2009, I began an IT-focused technical education and started programming. That was the beginning of my technical foundation, long before my current engineering leadership work.

To help finance my education in the United States, I sold T-shirts and won money in a televised challenge on Caldeirão do Huck. Those were practical ways of making further education possible.

I went on to Franklin & Marshall College, where I double majored in Computer Science and Economics and graduated in 2017.

That combination is part of how I approach founders’ decisions today: technical choices sit alongside costs, limited resources, and business priorities. I work from the constraints that are actually there.

TEACHING & SOCIAL IMPACT

Helping people become capable builders.

During 2016–2017, I taught programming at Children Deserve a Chance Foundation. I also helped create Attollo Code.

Teaching matters directly to the Founder Build Sprint. I use clear explanations, independent assignments, and useful feedback so you can understand your work, make progress between sessions, and become a more independent builder.

BEFORE WE START

A little more clarity.

Good engagements start with shared expectations.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. The Build Sprint is designed to help nontechnical founders get started. You do need time to learn, experiment, and build independently between sessions.

Will you build my application for me?+

You own the building process during a sprint. I provide instruction, reviews, and technical guidance. Advisory may include targeted investigation within the agreed scope, rather than ongoing implementation.

What happens if my project is complex?+

We narrow the scope to a useful learning or validation milestone. Three weeks may be one step in a longer journey; a finished or production-ready product is not guaranteed.

Can I renew a sprint?+

Yes, by mutual agreement after reviewing your progress, priorities, and availability. Renewal is not automatic.

What’s the difference between the two services?+

Choose a Build Sprint to learn and build your own product. Choose Technical Advisory for independent senior judgment on software, architecture, risk, or engineering decisions.

Can you review software built using AI?+

Yes. Focused architecture and codebase reviews can help identify risks and prioritize next steps. A focused review is not a comprehensive security audit or a certification of production readiness.

Do you offer ongoing support?+

Technical Advisory is available month-to-month, within up to four advisor hours per month. Engagements exclude on-call support, unlimited debugging, day-to-day management, and production ownership.

How does scheduling work?+

We agree on session times, the review cadence, and communication expectations before starting. Availability is limited; there is no promise of immediate or around-the-clock responses.
